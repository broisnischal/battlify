Add a full-screen **dot-matrix charging animation** (Nothing-phone style). When you
plug in, a pixel/dot-matrix battery fills to your current charge across the whole
screen — with a bright pulse sweeping up through the filled region and the percentage
in a 5×7 dot font — then dismisses after a few seconds (or on click / Esc). It's a
pure SwiftUI `Canvas`, no assets. Toggle it under Settings › Charging animation, with a
Preview button.
